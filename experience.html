Customer Care & Guidance

Style & Shopping Advice

Tap or hover for guidance on styling, garment preservation, and Boston pickup options.

Layering is essential for Boston’s changing coastal climate. Begin with neutral base layers like our Back Bay oversized knit polo, pair with relaxed denim, and drape a camel coat or lightweight knit for an effortless transition from daytime city strolls to crisp evenings.

Preserve fabric integrity by washing denim inside out in cold water and air-drying flat. For textured knitwear, use a gentle wool cycle or hand wash with mild detergent, and store folded to maintain the relaxed silhouette and prevent stretching.

When styling a neutral capsule wardrobe, allow one standout accessory to lead. A polished Commonwealth watch adds sophistication to casual knitwear, while amber-framed Seaport sunglasses bring warmth to cooler autumn tones without cluttering your look.

Filter directly by maximum budget on the Home page, explore complete outfit pairings in the Lookbook, and enjoy complimentary U.S. shipping on orders over $150 with convenient Boston pickup options.
